Scoring logic

Batters: 40% strike-rate uplift, 20% boundary-rate uplift, 20% dot-ball reduction and 20% dismissal-rate reduction.

Bowlers: 35% economy improvement, 25% wicket-rate improvement, 20% dot-ball increase and 20% boundary-rate reduction.

Metrics are z-standardised across eligible players. The weighted score is adjusted for pressure balls and distinct pressure matches. The score is a relative index, not a probability.

Baselines are phase-matched (powerplay 1–6, middle 7–15, death 16–20) and partially pooled toward the phase-wide average when player baseline samples are small.
